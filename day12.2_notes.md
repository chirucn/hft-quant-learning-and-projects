✅ DAY 12 – FINAL VERIFIED ANSWERS
1️. What is tick data?
    Every single trade and quote update in the market.
2️. What is a buyer-initiated trade?
    A trade that occurs at the ask price.
3️. Why is micro-price better than mid-price for HFT?
    Because it is liquidity-weighted and reflects short-term pressure instead of assuming a 50–50 balance.
4️. When can micro-price fail?
    When the order book changes suddenly due to cancellations, low liquidity, or hidden orders.
