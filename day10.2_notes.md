1️⃣ What happens to the order book when a market buy order arrives?

2️⃣ If a market buy size is larger than the best ask quantity, what happens to price?

3️⃣ Why does larger order size increase slippage?

4️⃣ Why do HFT firms prefer limit orders instead of market orders?

•	A market buy order hits the best ask, consumes liquidity, and if the size is large, the price moves up.
•	If the market buy size is larger than the best ask quantity, it consumes the best ask and moves to the next best ask, causing price impact.
•	Larger order size increases slippage because the available quantity at the best ask is limited; once it is exhausted, the order walks up to worse prices (next asks).
•	HFT firms prefer limit orders to control execution price and reduce slippage.
