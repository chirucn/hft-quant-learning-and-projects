1️. What does a positive order book imbalance indicate?
    A positive order book imbalance indicates stronger buying pressure than selling pressure and suggests short-term upward price pressure (bullish).
2️. What does a negative order book imbalance indicate?
    A negative order book imbalance indicates stronger selling pressure than buying pressure and suggests short-term downward price pressure (bearish).
3️. Why does order book imbalance work only on very short time horizons?
    Because the limit order book changes extremely fast due to order submissions, cancellations, and trades, so the imbalance signal quickly disappears.
4️. Can order book imbalance alone be used as a profitable HFT strategy? Why or why not?
    No. Order book imbalance alone is not sufficient because it is noisy and short-lived; it must be combined with other factors such as spread, queue position, recent trades, latency, and risk controls to be profitable.
